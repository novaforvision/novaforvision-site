Process

A project with NOVA,step by step

From the first message to the delivered file, you know where the film stands and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You write to us: the idea, the brand or the track, the target date. We come back to you with our questions.

    You
  2. 02

    Quote

    Within 48 hours: scope, deliverables, schedule and price. Nothing starts without your approval.

    NOVA
  3. 03

    Moodboard and storyboard

    We set the direction: references, look, shot-by-shot breakdown. Production starts once the storyboard is approved.

    Together
  4. 04

    Production

    AI generation, 3D, a shoot if the project calls for it, then editing, visual effects and sound. You follow progress at each checkpoint.

    NOVA
  5. 05

    Feedback

    You respond to a first version, we adjust. The number of revision rounds is set in the quote.

    Together
  6. 06

    Delivery

    The final files, in the formats agreed for each release.

    NOVA
