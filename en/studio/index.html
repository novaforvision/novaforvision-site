Studio

NOVA is an independent post-production, visual effects and AI creation studio based in Liège, Belgium. It makes AI ad campaigns, VFX and edits for brands, agencies and artists, in Belgium, France and beyond. Its hybrid workflow brings together generative AI, 3D and compositing, from first exploration to final shot: giving shape to your vision.

Team

  • Mikail Yildirim

    Co-founder

    VFX, editing and sound design

    Music videos are where it all started for me. That is where I built my experience in editing, visual effects and sound, working with artists to give shape to their world. Self-taught, I like to keep a hand on every part of a video: its rhythm, its look, its mood. Today, artificial intelligence (AI) is part of my practice and opens new ways to imagine and produce more ambitious projects.

  • Adem Yildirim

    Co-founder

    Graphic design and photography

    An architect by training, I built a parallel practice as a self-taught graphic designer and photographer. I have worked for YouTube, Twitch and content creators, always with the same standard: images that catch the eye and carry a recognisable identity. My background combines a sense of composition, web culture and an eye for detail. Today, I bring these influences together in marketing campaigns and audiovisual projects where art direction takes centre stage.
